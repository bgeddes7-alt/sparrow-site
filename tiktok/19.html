Your hand is dead. 4 things beginners get wrong.

What actually happens, per the rulebook.

MYTH
Your hand's dead, so you don't pay.
THE RULE

You still pay the winner.

A dead hand can't win, but it pays like everyone else.

MYTH
Know your own hand is dead? Say so.
THE RULE

Keep quiet and keep playing.

It's only dead once another player calls it. Play defense until then.

MYTH
You can call someone dead on a hunch.
THE RULE

Only from tiles everyone can see.

Exposures on racks and the discards. Nothing else.

MYTH
Dead hands keep drawing and throwing.
THE RULE

Once it's called, you stop.

No more picks, no more discards. You sit out the rest of the game.

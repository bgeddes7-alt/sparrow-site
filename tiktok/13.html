4 mahj "rules" your table might have wrong.

Know the real ones before Thursday.

MYTH
Flowers are just bonus tiles.
THE RULE

Flowers are part of your hand.

In American mahjong they sit right on the card. Any flower fills an F.

MYTH
Changed your mind? Grab your discard back.
THE RULE

Once it's named or touches the table, it's gone.

So think before you say it out loud.

MYTH
Throw a joker and it can be anything.
THE RULE

A thrown joker copies the discard before it.

You call it as that same tile.

MYTH
Swap a tile for a joker to grab the discard you need.
THE RULE

Not for that discard.

You can't trade for a joker just to claim the last discard, for an exposure or for Mahjong.

Two people call the same tile. Who gets it?

5 calling questions. Guess before you swipe.

Question 1
East throws a 6 Bam. The two players after her both call it for a pung. Who gets it?
6BAM
NEXT IN TURN

The player closest to the discarder.

Counting in turn order from whoever threw it. Unless the other player already started exposing their tiles.

Question 2
Same 6 Bam. One player wants it for a pung. The other needs it for Mahjong. Now who?
MAHJONG

Mahjong wins the tile.

A call for Mahjong beats a call for an exposure, every time.

Question 3
You hesitated. The next player already drew and racked her tile. Can you still call that discard?
NO

Too late.

You can call a tile from the moment it's thrown until the next player racks the tile she drew.

Question 4
The tile you need got thrown three turns ago. Can you call it now?

Only the newest discard.

Once another tile lands on top, the old one is out of reach.

Question 5
You set out your pung and realize you grabbed the wrong tiles. You haven't discarded yet. Can you fix it?
YES

Until you discard.

Fix your exposure during that same turn. Once you discard, it's locked for the rest of the game.

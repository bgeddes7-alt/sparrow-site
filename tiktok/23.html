You won. Who pays what? 4 myths.

How the payout really works, per the rulebook.

MYTH
Everyone pays you the same.
THE RULE

Whoever threw your tile pays double.

Everyone else pays the hand's value from the card.

MYTH
Picking it off the wall is a normal win.
THE RULE

Self-picked? Everyone pays double.

Nobody threw it, so all three players pay twice the value.

MYTH
A joker swap for the win doesn't count.
THE RULE

It counts as self-picked.

Win by exchanging for a joker and all three pay double.

MYTH
No jokers in your hand? No bonus.
THE RULE

Jokerless doubles it again.

Up to four times the value. Not for Singles and Pairs hands, which never use jokers.

Know your hand's value before you call it.

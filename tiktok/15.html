The night before mahj night. 15 minutes.

That's all it takes to walk in sharp.

Minutes 1 to 5

Redo the lesson that tripped you up.

Charleston, jokers, the card. Pick the one you'd least like to be asked about.

Minutes 5 to 12

Play one practice hand.

Make every call yourself. Then check what the expert would have done.

Minutes 12 to 15

Ask the one thing you won't ask out loud.

The expert gives you the move and the reason. Nobody's watching.

Before you leave

Pack your card.

Everyone plays from their own. Circle two hands you'd love to make and walk in with a plan.

Show up ready. Not perfect.

Words on the mahj card nobody explains.

Pung, Kong, Quint, and that little C.

"Pair."
Two of the same tile. Jokers can't be used in a pair. You need the real thing.
"Pung."
Three of the same tile. This is where jokers start to help.
"Kong."
Four of the same tile. Jokers can fill in here too.
"Quint."
Five of the same. There are only four of each number tile, so five 3 Dots means at least one joker.
"Sextet."
Six of the same. With number tiles, that means at least two jokers.
"C"
A concealed hand. You can't call a discard for it unless that tile wins you Mahjong.
"25"
The number at the end of each line is what the hand pays. 25 is the lowest a winning hand can be worth.

Now the card makes sense.

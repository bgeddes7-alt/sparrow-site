Can you call it? 5 questions from mahj night.

Guess before you swipe. Keep score.

Question 1
You need one more 5 Dot for a pair. Someone throws a 5 Dot. Can you call it?
5DOT
?DOT
ONLY TO WIN

Only if it's your Mahjong tile.

Otherwise you never call a discard for a pair. Calls are for pungs, kongs and quints.

Question 2
Your hand on the card has a C next to it. Someone throws the tile you need for a pung. Call it?
NO

C means concealed.

A concealed hand can only call one discard: the tile that wins it.

Question 3
Someone exposed three 7 Craks, one of them a joker. You have a 7 Crak. Can you take the joker?

YES

On your turn.

Swap your real 7 Crak for their joker. It works whether you just drew the tile or already had it.

Question 4

NO

Jokers never go in pairs or singles.

Only in groups of three or more of the same tile.

Question 5
You just called a discard. Where does it go?
ON TOP

Face up on top of your rack.

Set it out with the tiles it goes with. Slip it into your rack instead and your hand is dead.

Get it wrong at home. Not at the table.

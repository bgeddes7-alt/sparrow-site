The Charleston, start to finish.

The part every beginner dreads, in 5 slides.

1

Right, across, left.

The first Charleston always happens. You pass 3 tiles each time.

← Left
2

The last pass can be blind.

On the left pass, you can pass along up to 3 of the tiles coming to you without looking at them.

3

Round two only happens if everyone agrees.

Same passes, reversed: left, across, right.

← Left
Across ↑
Right →
4

Then a courtesy pass.

Optional. You and the player across from you swap 0 to 3 tiles. You agree on how many.

5

Never pass a joker.

Jokers stay with you through every pass.
